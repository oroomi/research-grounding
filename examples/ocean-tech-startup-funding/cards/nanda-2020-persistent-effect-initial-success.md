## Summary

> Sentences copied from the abstract, not paraphrased; we have not read the full paper.

We examined persistence in the performance of venture capital (VC) firms at the level of individual investments underlying fund returns. We found that, while initial success strongly predicted long-term success, the distribution of investments in state-industry-year cells accounted for roughly half of this performance persistence. Our results suggest that venture capital firms exhibit performance persistence because early success affords them greater access to deal flow in subsequent investments, thereby perpetuating differences in the outcomes of initial investments.
