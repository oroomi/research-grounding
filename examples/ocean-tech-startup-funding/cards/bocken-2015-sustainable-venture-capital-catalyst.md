## Summary

> Sentences copied from the abstract, not paraphrased; we have not read the full paper.

This paper provides insight into how venture capitalists can contribute to sustainable business success, by investigating their role, motivations, investment theses, and barriers and enablers to success of sustainable ventures. It was found that next to financial support, venture capitalists provide triple bottom line business advice and network support. Sustainable start-ups should focus on triple bottom line business model innovation, find opportunity in new technology and funding platforms and develop multiple business cases to create success beyond the ‘green customer base’.
