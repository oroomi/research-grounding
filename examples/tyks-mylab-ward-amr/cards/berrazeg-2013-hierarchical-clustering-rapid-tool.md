## Summary

> Sentences copied from the abstract, not paraphrased; we have not read the full paper.

The aim of this study was to develop a rapid hierarchical clustering bioinformatic tool for application on antibiotic susceptibility testing (AST) results (resistant, intermediate, sensitive) of a series of Klebsiella pneumoniae clinical isolates from Algeria and from France for surveillance of antibiotic-resistance phenotypes. Hierarchical clustering results were compared to standard resistance phenotypes to evaluate the accuracy of the method. This tool could be easily implemented in routine clinical microbiology laboratories.

## What it measured / predicts

From the abstract: Antimicrobial resistance is on the rise, and its early detection and surveillance are critical to implement effective control measures. Hierarchical clustering by the MeV software applied to the AST results for all 1011 isolates generated clusters that were significantly representative of phenotypic classification and geographical origin, in less than 1 min.
