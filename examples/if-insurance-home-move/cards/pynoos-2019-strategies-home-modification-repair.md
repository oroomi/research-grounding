Home modification and repair have emerged as public policy and service delivery issues because they can help older people age in place. In order to increase the incidence of home modification and repair, improvements need to be made in assessing homes, expanding public awareness, and developing programs. Strategies to increase the incidence of home modification and repair include more widespread and systematic home assessments, increased public awareness about the role of the environment, and the creation of more programs that provide affordable home modification and repair services.
