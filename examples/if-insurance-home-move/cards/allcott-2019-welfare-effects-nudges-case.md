## Summary

> Sentences copied from the abstract, not paraphrased; we have not read the full paper.

We combine a field experiment with a simple theoretical framework to evaluate the welfare effects of one especially policy-relevant intervention, home energy social comparison reports. In our sample, the reports increase social welfare, although traditional evaluation approaches overstate gains because they ignore significant costs incurred by nudge recipients. We develop a prediction algorithm for optimal targeting; this approach would double the welfare gains.
